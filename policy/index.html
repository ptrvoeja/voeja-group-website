Your privacy is foundational to everything we build.

This Privacy Policy explains how VOEJA Group and its subsidiaries — VOEJA Travel, VOEJA Finance, and VOEJA Tech — collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Who We Are

VOEJA Group ("we", "our", "us") is the parent organization of a multi-sector strategic group headquartered in the United States, operating globally through three subsidiaries: VOEJA Travel (travel and experiences), VOEJA Finance (capital and advisory services), and VOEJA Tech (digital infrastructure and software).

This policy applies to all VOEJA Group websites, applications, and services under the voeja.us and voeja.group domains.

2. Data We Collect

We collect information in the following categories:

person Identity & Contact Data

Full name, email address, phone number, billing address, company name, and job title — collected when you register, make a booking, or contact us.

travel_explore Travel & Service Data

Travel preferences, itinerary details, passport information (where required by law), and transactional records related to bookings or advisory engagements.

monitoring Usage & Technical Data

IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, session duration, referral URLs, and device identifiers — collected automatically via cookies and analytics tools.

payments Financial Data

Payment card details are processed exclusively by PCI-DSS-compliant third-party processors. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your data only for lawful purposes:

  • check_circleService delivery — fulfilling bookings, delivering consulting services, and providing technical support.
  • check_circleAccount management — creating and managing your account, authenticating your identity.
  • check_circleCommunications — responding to inquiries, sending service notifications, and (with consent) marketing updates.
  • check_circleLegal compliance — meeting obligations under applicable law, including anti-money-laundering (AML) regulations relevant to VOEJA Finance.
  • check_circleProduct improvement — analyzing usage patterns to improve our platforms and services.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • arrow_rightService providers acting as data processors on our behalf (e.g., cloud hosting, payment gateways, CRM platforms), bound by strict data processing agreements.
  • arrow_rightTravel partners & airlines strictly to the extent needed to fulfil your bookings.
  • arrow_rightRegulatory authorities when legally required or to protect the rights, property, or safety of VOEJA Group or others.
  • arrow_rightAcquirers in the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

5. International Transfers

VOEJA Group operates globally. Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence, including the United States and countries within the European Economic Area. Where required, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, or equivalent legal mechanisms, to ensure an adequate level of protection.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Travel booking records are typically retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance. Financial advisory records may be retained for up to 10 years in accordance with applicable regulations. Marketing preferences are held until you withdraw consent. Upon expiry, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access

Request a copy of the data we hold about you.

Rectification

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

Erasure

Request deletion of your data where no legal obligation requires us to retain it.

Portability

Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

Objection

Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.

Restriction

Request that we limit the processing of your data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, submit a request using our Data Rights Request Form or contact us at lgpd@voeja.us. We will respond within 15 calendar days.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including TLS encryption for data in transit, AES-256 encryption for data at rest, role-based access controls, regular security audits, and incident response procedures. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; however, we are committed to continuous improvement of our security posture.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at lgpd@voeja.us and we will take steps to delete such data promptly.

10. Contact Us

For any questions, concerns, or requests related to this Privacy Policy, please reach out to our Data Protection team:

VOEJA Group — Privacy Team

Email: lgpd@voeja.us

Form: Data Rights Request Form

Response Time

We aim to respond to all privacy requests within 15 calendar days.

We use cookies to make our services work and to improve your experience.

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use, and how you can control them.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognize your device and remember information about your visit. Similar technologies — such as pixel tags, web beacons, and local storage — work in analogous ways and are governed by this same policy.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary

Always Active

Essential for the website to function. They enable core features like authentication, security, and session management. These cannot be disabled.

Analytics & Performance

Optional

Help us understand how visitors interact with our websites by collecting anonymous usage data. We use tools such as Google Analytics. This data is aggregated and does not identify individuals.

Functional

Optional

Remember your preferences (language, region, display settings) to provide a more personalized experience across sessions.

Marketing & Targeting

Optional

Used to track your browsing activity across websites to deliver targeted advertising and measure the effectiveness of our campaigns. These are only set with your explicit consent.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our sites are placed by third parties. We work with the following categories of third-party services that may set cookies:

  • arrow_rightAnalytics: Google Analytics, Hotjar
  • arrow_rightScheduling & CRM: Zoho (including our booking platform at book.voeja.us)
  • arrow_rightSocial media: LinkedIn (for embedded follow widgets and campaign tracking)
  • arrow_rightPayment processing: Stripe, PayPal (limited to transactional contexts)

These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies. We encourage you to review them.

4. Your Control Over Cookies

You have several options to manage cookies:

Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies via their settings. Note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may impair service functionality. Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions.

Opt-Out Tools

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. For interest-based advertising, visit the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.

Consent Banner

When you first visit our website, a cookie consent banner allows you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You may update your preferences at any time by clearing your cookies and revisiting the site.
