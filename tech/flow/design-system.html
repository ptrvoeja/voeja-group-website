Overview

Move Money at the Speed of Life.

Next-generation card programs and global money transfers, built for the modern entrepreneur. This is the design system that keeps the product premium, global, and tech-forward — from the glassmorphism hero to the very last status badge.

Logo

Primary lockup — gradient icon + FLOW wordmark. Use on dark surfaces (#0a0b0d / #0e0f12).

Asset: images/flow-financial-logo.png

Personality

Premium · Global · Tech-forward · Accessible. Trust from the deep forest green, energy from lime, and technology from azure.

Premium · Global · Tech-forward · Accessible. Confidence from the modern near-black, energy from lime, and technology from azure.

Tone of voice

Confident, dynamic, transparent. “Zero hidden fees,” speed and control in every piece of microcopy.

Audience

Global entrepreneurs — users of FlowCard (debit) and Flow Transfers (remittances).

The three products
FlowCard

Premium debit card — real-time tracking, virtual cards, and multi-currency support.

Flow Transfers

Global transfers — 180+ countries, flat $0.99 fee, average delivery under 3 min.

Dashboard

Balance, charts, and recent transactions in real time.
